\section{Conclusion}

\begin{frame}{Conclusion}
    \footnotesize
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Proposed a \textbf{lightweight, anonymous, decoupled} distributed authentication
        scheme for multihop IoT, extending DAuth.
        \item \textbf{Per-session pseudonym rotation} prevents adversarial cross-session tracking --
        $ID_D$ never appears on the open channel.
        \item \textbf{Dual-state synchronisation} gives transparent desynchronisation recovery
        \emph{without re-enrolment}.
        \item PUF-based secrets + hash accumulator + XOR masking keep overhead low.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{0.15cm}
    \begin{block}{Validated results}
        \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{ProVerif}: all queries satisfied (correspondence, secrecy, forward secrecy, anonymity).
            \item \textbf{COOJA}: \textbf{42.8\%} / \textbf{32.4\%} lower per-device energy than LAAKA / Zhou.
            \item \textbf{Hardware (RPi 4B)}: 0.286\,J/round -- \textbf{52.5\%} / \textbf{14.2\%} below LAAKA / Zhou.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
\end{frame}
